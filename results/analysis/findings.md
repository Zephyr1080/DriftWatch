# DriftWatch: findings

_Generated by `scripts/analyze_results.py` from `results/batch_results.csv`. All numbers come from simulated drift on the Bike Sharing data._

## Setup

- 20 streams per condition, drift starting at batch 15; batches of 500 rows.
- Thresholds calibrated for a 5% false-alarm rate: PSI 0.0854, KS 0.1085.
- Achieved false-alarm rate on fresh stable batches: PSI 3%, KS 6%.
- Drift counts as confirmed after 2 alerting batches in a row. On clean control streams that happens by chance in 0% (PSI) and 10% (KS) of streams.

- **Missing values, low**: PSI confirmed drift in 75% of streams vs 15% for KS (paired exact test p = 0.004).
- Both detectors confirm drift in at least 95% of streams for: covariate shift (weather) (low), covariate shift (weather) (medium), covariate shift (weather) (high), hour shift (medium), hour shift (high), noise (medium), noise (high), missing values (medium), missing values (high). - Missing values, medium: PSI confirmed drift earlier (mean delay PSI 1.0 vs KS 2.5 batches).

## Real (natural) drift in 2012

- PSI alerted on 100% and KS on 100% of the 13 natural-period batches.
- random forest: accuracy 0.913 in the drifted period vs 0.899 on stable data.
- svm: accuracy 0.907 in the drifted period vs 0.847 on stable data.
- logistic regression: accuracy 0.823 in the drifted period vs 0.777 on stable data.
- So the inputs drifted strongly while accuracy did not fall: an alert is a reason to investigate, not proof the model is broken.

## Limitations

- Only about 175 stable days exist, so thresholds depend on which days are used (a threshold calibrated on one set of days can give a very different false-alarm rate on another). Calibration therefore used the same stable pool as the experiments, and batches reuse rows; treat results as a controlled demonstration.
- Drift is simulated, one kind at a time, with fixed severity levels; real drift is messier. Labels never change (no concept drift).
- Both detectors use the maximum score over 11 features; other aggregations could change the ranking.
- 20 streams per condition gives coarse rates. With 12 drifted conditions about one p < 0.05 is expected by chance.
- The accuracy comparison uses a 0.03 damage cutoff chosen by us.
